How it works

From first message to first payout

You pay nothing at any step. You approve the design before anything goes live.

More questions? Read the FAQ or get in touch.

  1. Apply

    Day 1

    Tell us about your stream with the form on this page. We look at every channel and reply within a few days.

  2. Design it together

    Days 2 to 3

    We sketch your slot from your emotes, jokes and colours. You get two rounds of changes.

  3. We build it

    Days 4 to 10

    Maths, art, sound and animation, tested so every spin is fair.

  4. Launch on Stake

    Week 2

    It goes live through Engine, and you open it with a launch stream.

  5. Get paid

    Every month

    Your share of the royalty arrives monthly, with the numbers on your own dashboard.
