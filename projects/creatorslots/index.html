Live · my main project

CreatorSlots

CreatorSlots builds custom slot games for Kick streamers: their name on the reels, their emotes as symbols and a bonus round built on their community’s running jokes. The game goes live on Stake, costs the streamer nothing and pays them a share of the royalty every month. 18+.

From stream to slot

A streamer fills in one form. We sketch their slot from their emotes, colours and running jokes, they get two rounds of changes, and within one to two weeks the game is live on Stake through Engine.

A deal you read like a paytable

The homepage lays out the whole arrangement the way a slot shows its payouts: $0 upfront, 40 to 50% of the royalty, paid monthly, the same RTP for everyone. A slider estimates what a slot could earn from the streamer’s viewer count.

Behind the scenes

A dashboard follows every streamer from pitch to signature: opened, clicked, demos played, channel growth. Pitches and follow-ups go out automatically, in English, Spanish or Portuguese, and stop on their own at a no.

Built to be safe

Every page runs only its own scripts, every table has row-level security, and sensitive actions need 2FA. What visitors fill in only reaches the people who need to answer it.
