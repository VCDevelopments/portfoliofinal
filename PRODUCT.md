# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Startups and founders who need an MVP or prototype built quickly.
- Agencies and studios looking for a freelance developer and designer.
- Employers and recruiters hiring for a job or internship.

They meet the work on vcdevelopments.info, on LinkedIn (where the case-study videos autoplay muted in the feed) and through shared project links.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio of Lothar Van Cauwenbergh (VCDevelopments): it shows websites, iOS apps and AI tools he designed and built, and turns visitors into inquiries. Success is someone getting in touch (the inquiry form, email info.vcdevelopments@gmail.com, or LinkedIn) after trying the work.

## Positioning

One person who designs and builds the whole thing, end to end; working prototypes early instead of decks and mockups; premium craft. The site proves it by being the work: every project runs as a working demo you can use on the page.

## Operating Context

- A scroll-driven Three.js site in four chapters (neon logo in a snowy valley, projects on live 3D iPhones and a MacBook, a blueprint gyroscope with services, a particle finale with contact).
- Each project has a pop-up with a full-size working demo and guided tour, a changelog and a shareable project page.
- English and Dutch.
- Case-study videos (4:5) for LinkedIn and link previews, recorded from the demos' guided tours.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages, no build step; every change is shown to the owner locally and only pushed live after approval.
- Demos never send anything: the CreatorSlots copy has its application form and logging switched off.
- Analytics are cookieless (Umami) and switched off until the owner provides a website ID.

## Brand Commitments

- Name VCDevelopments, the "VC" mark (a V stroke and a C arc), shown as a neon tube on the site.
- Voice: plain, direct, first person, no hype.
- Contact: info.vcdevelopments@gmail.com, LinkedIn lotharvancauwenbergh, GitHub VCDevelopments.

## Evidence on Hand

- Two projects: CreatorSlots (creatorslots.net, the main project: custom slot games for Kick streamers on Stake, 18+) and VCDevelopments (this site). Data in `js/projects.js`; demos in `demos/`.
- Changelogs come from the CreatorSlots night log and the portfolio's git history.
- No client testimonials, client logos, metrics or prices exist; never invent them. Service timelines on the site are the owner's to confirm.

## Product Principles

- Show, don't claim: let the work run in front of the viewer.
- Working beats pretty: every visual serves a real, usable thing.
- Fast to the point: something happens immediately.
- One maker's hand: consistent craft across site, demos and videos.
