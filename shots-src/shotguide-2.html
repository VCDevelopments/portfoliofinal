STEPS
  1. Shoot within the hour before sunset, facing the sun.
  2. Place your subject a third in from the left, sun just beside them.
  3. Hold the phone at chest height, tilted slightly down; keep the horizon level.
  4. Expose for the sky so the figure becomes a silhouette.
